Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

StudyAI Tutor ("we," "us," "our") operates the StudyAI Tutor website and tutoring service (the "Service"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your actual password in readable form).
  • Usage data: the subjects you study, the questions you ask Eze, and your conversation history, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Uploaded images: homework photos you attach are sent to our AI provider to generate an answer; we do not permanently store the image itself after the response is generated.
  • Payment information: subscription payments are handled entirely by our payment processor. We never see or store your card details — only whether your subscription is active.
  • Approximate location: we use your IP address to estimate your country, so we can show pricing in a currency close to your own. We do not store your precise location or your IP address long-term.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see the "Cookies and advertising" section below.

How we use your information

  • To provide tutoring responses and save your study history across sessions.
  • To enforce free-plan daily limits and manage Premium subscriptions.
  • To process payments and detect fraud or abuse.
  • To maintain and improve the Service, and to communicate with you about your account.

Third parties we share data with

We work with a small number of third-party providers, each with their own privacy practices:

  • AI model provider (Google Gemini): your questions and any uploaded images are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate tutoring responses.
  • Our payment processor: processes all subscription payments; handles your card details directly, not us.
  • ipinfo.io: used to estimate your country from your IP address, for currency-display purposes only.
  • Google AdSense / Google Ads: may be used to display advertising on the Service — see the section below for details.

None of these providers are authorized to use your data for purposes beyond providing their specific service to us.

Cookies and advertising

We and third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also control cookies generally through your browser's settings, though disabling cookies may affect how the Service functions.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and chat history for as long as your account is active, so your study progress is preserved. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, email us at the address below and we will process your request. Note: password recovery is not currently available (see our sign-up page for details), so please keep your login details somewhere safe.

Children's privacy

StudyAI Tutor is intended for students of school age using the Service with the involvement of a parent, guardian, or teacher where appropriate. We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children than is necessary to provide the Service. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information inappropriately, please contact us and we will address it.

Data security

Passwords are hashed using industry-standard methods before storage. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable, standard measures to protect your information.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below.
